Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before you enquire about a gift.

ORDERS & PERSONALISATION
Can I request a personalised hamper?

Yes. Tell us about the occasion, recipient, preferred colours, desired contents and personal touches. We will discuss what is possible based on product availability, budget and timing.

Can you source products that are not shown on the website?

Please share the brand, model, specifications, quantity and budget. We will review sourcing options and availability. Every request remains subject to confirmation.

Can I include a gift message or company branding?

Tell us about any gift card, logo or branded packaging requirements when you enquire. Personalisation options depend on the design, quantity, preparation time and production feasibility.

What happens if a selected product is unavailable?

If an item is unavailable, we will discuss possible alternatives with you first. Substitutions are only made after your approval.

PRICING, PAYMENT & TIMING
How is hamper pricing determined?

Quotations are based on product selection, quantity, personalisation, packaging, sourcing and delivery. Share your budget range so we can discuss suitable options.

Which payment methods are available?

Payment methods and instructions will be confirmed directly by our team during quotation or order confirmation. Please verify payment details through our official contact channel before making a payment.

How much time should I allow for an order?

Preparation time depends on hamper contents, personalisation, quantity and product availability. Share your required date as early as possible so we can check feasibility.

DELIVERY & CORPORATE GIFTING
Can you deliver to my location?

Service area, delivery fees and estimated timing depend on the destination and order details. Share your city or postcode and preferred date so our team can confirm the options.

Can I order for a company or event?

Yes. We welcome corporate gifting enquiries for clients, teams, partners and business occasions. Please include the estimated quantity, budget range, delivery area, target date and any branding requirements.

How do I place an order?

Contact us through WhatsApp or email. Include the occasion, preferred gifts, quantity, budget, delivery area and required date. Our team will help you discuss the next steps.

Contact Anderson to discuss your gifting requirements.
