Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before you enquire about a gift.

Can I request a personalised hamper?

Yes. Tell us the occasion, recipient, preferred colours and personal touches you have in mind. We will discuss what is possible for your selected collection.

Can I order for a company or event?

Yes. We welcome corporate gifting enquiries. Include the estimated quantity, budget range, delivery area and target date.

How much does a hamper cost?

Pricing depends on selected items, presentation, personalisation and quantity. Contact us with your preferred budget range for options.

How do I enquire?

Use the enquiry form or contact us through WhatsApp. Your WhatsApp message opens as a draft for you to review before sending.
